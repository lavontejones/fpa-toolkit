# Demo walkthrough

1. Run `python fpa.py` and open the report.
2. January revenue is $8,000 favorable, direct costs are $3,000 unfavorable.
3. February's lower revenue prompts review of pipeline and pricing rather than automatic cost cuts.
4. Base monthly growth is 2%, downside −3%, upside 4%. First-month revenue is $110,000 in all scenarios, with $44,000 variable cost and $45,000 fixed cost: $21,000 operating surplus and $96,000 closing cash.
5. Compare final-month cash across scenarios. Change assumptions in a local copy and rerun.

The committed report is generated from fictional fixtures. No screenshots or client information are required to reproduce it.
